Questions

Common questions

Do you need access to my inboxes or sequencer?

No. Every check reads public DNS records. Nothing touches your mailboxes, and we never see email content.

My sequencer already has warm-up and health scores. Why do I need this?

Sequencer health scores cover the inboxes connected to that one tool. Senderwatch watches the domains themselves, across every tool and client, and tells you when DNS changes break them.

Why did DKIM show a warning when I know it is set up?

The free check tries the 12 most common selectors. If your provider uses a custom selector, add it in your account and the daily check will use it.
